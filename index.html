What a language actually gives you

Not a certificate on a shelf. Five things people come here for.

Speaking

Progress you can actually see

The game part is not decoration. It is what keeps repetition survivable.

A map, not a list

Every finished lesson opens a piece of the map. You see where you are and what is next — instead of a progress bar that means nothing.

Tested, not clicked through

A lesson ends with a test that gives no hints and does not show you the answer. So a result means something — unlike a streak, which only means you opened the app.

Play is the method

Coins, gear and quests exist to make repetition bearable. In practice you get the answer when you miss; in a test you do not — that is the whole difference between them.

Choose where to go first

You can switch later — progress stays on the map.
